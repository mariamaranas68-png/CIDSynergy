About the publication

Inform. Inspire. Collaborate.

CID Synergy is a sample online publication concept for the Curriculum Implementation Division of the Schools Division Office of Baybay City.

Our purpose

We share stories that recognize the work of educators, celebrate learners, document promising practices, and strengthen collaboration across schools. This prototype uses placeholder content and is designed to be adapted by an authorized publication administrator.

Our editorial values

Accuracy · Respect · Inclusion · Creativity · Service
